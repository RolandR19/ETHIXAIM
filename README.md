# ETHIXAIM 👁️‍🗨️

> **The messenger that refuses to remember you.**

Most apps promise encryption, then ask for your phone number, sync your contacts, and save your chat history on remote drives.

**ETHIXAIM** takes the opposite approach: zero accounts, zero cloud storage, zero persistence. If you close your browser tab, the conversation never existed.

---

### The Architecture of Amnesia

ETHIXAIM operates entirely within your browser's volatile memory (RAM). There are no databases, no sign-up forms, and no middlemen.

* **No Credentials:** No phone numbers, no emails, no passwords. Identity is an ephemeral cryptographic key generated on page load.
* **Pure P2P (WebRTC):** Direct device-to-device transport. Your messages never pass through a hosted server queue.
* **100% Volatile RAM:** Zero disk writes, zero local storage, zero cookies. 
* **60-Second Evaporation:** Messages detonate automatically after 60 seconds. A browser refresh (`F5`) purges the entire session permanently.
* **Deliberately Minimal:** Text, voice notes, and instant photos only. Video capture and heavy streaming features were deliberately removed to eliminate attack surfaces and keep payload sizes microscopic.
